# Character model audit - read-only

Reviewed the current game-size sprite/classic contact sheets, enlarged Golden Whisk composites, isolated base/blade atlases, art/blender/knight.py, art/blender/pack.py, index.html compositing and game/collection-art.js. No runtime or asset edits made.

## Largest visual issues

1. **Mixed anatomy and visual style (compositing).** The baked base contains both gloves/arms and the shield. Active attacks and earned weapons add a separately positioned procedural arm and red gauntlet. The silver diagonal previously questioned is an armored forearm, not a second sword, but its straight long tube reads like another weapon and the retained brown glove reads like another hand. The procedural outline is much thicker than the baked outline. A better bake alone will not fix this.
2. **Face loses personality at actual size (model).** Most helm variants share a rounded lathed dome. The elevated camera compresses a narrow visor into a dark strip with tiny eye/rivet highlights. The excellent large-eye idea in the source is not surviving projection and downsampling. Larger, separated eye shapes on a flatter front visor plane are more valuable than more geometric detail.
3. **Too much fine armor/custard detail for too little silhouette (model).** Multiple bands, small cuffs, rivets, tiny buckle and seven thin custard drips form bright/dark noise. Team color breaks into shoulder circles and a short, obscured torso band. Boots merge beneath the body. Use fewer broad planes and a larger unbroken tabard/team patch, with a readable gap between broad boots.
4. **Cosmetics float or remain billboards while the body turns (compositing).** Rooster Crown and Rice Guard attach to an approximate headY rather than the modeled crown surface; the baked custard remains under them. Their face-forward shape changes only slightly in profile. Capes hang from a generic torso location and do not share the bake's perspective. Strong authored silhouettes are present, but they need the same projected attachment and material treatment as the body.
5. **Baked softness versus vector sharpness (pipeline).** A 160px cell is drawn at 0.66 scale before the overall 1.12 factor. Sheet rendering lowers temporal AA to eight samples, then packs lossy WebP quality92. Thin high-contrast details blur together; heavy 2D overlays stay crisp. Supersampling and a lossless trial can improve edges, but will not repair proportion and attachment problems.

## Proposed coherent direction

A small, broad kitchen knight with a faceted pot-like helmet, flat dark visor and two expressive cream eyes. One large custard dollop and two deliberate drips establish the identity; keep a plume as an optional silhouette accent. Broad team-colored tabard, rounded shoulders, dark mittens and separated oversized boots give three clear masses: head, body, feet. Use warm cream highlights, restrained blue-purple shadows, one consistent dark-plum outline weight and limited interior lines. Weapons should have chunky readable heads and compact articulated arms. Keep the existing camera initially; make front planes and features survive that camera before changing projection.

This should improve every default knight before adding decorative detail. Golden Whisk remains an actual whisk, Toast remains toast, and each helmet keeps a distinct outline. Cosmetic ownership and stable save IDs must remain unchanged.

## Integration constraints for the prototype

- Current runtime expects CK_SPRITES.knight with cell, per, anims, helms[h].base/mask/metal, plumes, blades, cape, pats, embs, anchorY and topY. Layers are dataURL atlases plus [x,y,width,height,offsetX,offsetY] per frame. Five stored directions S/SE/E/NE/N; west-facing views mirror these.
- Existing animation layout is idle2, walk6, swing4, block2, hit1, win2 (17 frames per direction). Preserve that contract for a first drop-in model. New named attack animations can be optional fields later, with existing metadata-driven fallback.
- Keep 160px canonical cells initially. Current sprScale(r)=r/22*0.66 and main draw assumes that canonical size. Simply changing main cell to320 doubles character scale. If increasing resolution, normalize both draw scale and sprTop to a160px reference, including the hero renderer path.
- AnchorY/topY currently come from the south idle alpha bounds rather than explicit model sockets. Export a stable projected ground origin and per-frame shoulder, hand, helm-top and cape attachment positions for a better pipeline. Keep the same world ground pivot so shadows, feet, Steve riding lift, falling and collision radius stay coherent.
- Separate the weapon-side arm/hand from base, or provide an explicit arm-free base variant for procedural weapons. Suppress the corresponding baked arm when using an overlay. Ideally export front/back limb layers and attachment-depth flags, so one hand belongs to one weapon throughout all directions.
- Export custom helmet-compatible base variants or separate custard/top decoration. Otherwise crown/bowl headgear stacks over baked custard. Keep hero/menu and gameplay geometry consistent to avoid an obvious model change at match start.
- Re-bake related occluded layers together. Plumes/capes/blades/emblems contain holdouts from the existing body, helmet and sword. Swapping only the base leaves old-shaped holes, protruding fragments or missing pixels. Team and metal masks must align exactly with their new base frames.
- Preserve all existing kit IDs and the hero atlas fallback while loading. A prototype should be selected by explicit visual metadata/feature choice, never by renaming earned item IDs. No changes to e.r, sizeMul, reach, SWINGS, damage windows, movement, input or net entity identity are necessary.
- Keep procedural animation transforms around the new canonical pivot and ensure helmet/cape/weapon layers share them. The 60ms startup/end exclusion remains authoritative. Shape and attachment work should not change combat timing or hitstop.
- Evaluate consistent1.5-2px outer edges at final gameplay scale, fewer interior lines,32+ render samples or2x supersampling, and lossless WebP as measured options. Inspect alpha-edge halos and decoded atlas memory; do not assume a higher resolution alone means better quality.

## Prototype acceptance before full rebake

Compare default knight at native gameplay size in south, east and north views on a real arena, then a larger turntable. Test red/blue/gold/dark tint, a black silhouette, Golden Whisk + both earned helms, light/heavy/stab/bash poses, block, hit, ride-Steve, and menu-to-match continuity. Confirm one visible weapon hand, readable eyes/team patch, grounded feet, no cropped plume/weapon, and correct behind-body occlusion. Inspect all eight knights together at normal camera scale; a zoomed hero alone is insufficient. Use the existing collision and layer-suppression checks and measure warm rendering cost/decoded memory separately.
