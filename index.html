How to play

FightSwing to bonk. Hold Swing for a charged heavy that breaks blocks. Block stops swords and bounces arrows back; block just as they swing to parry and stun them. Holding block drains your guard. Swing while blocking to shield-bash. Dash hops pits and dodges; attack right after a dash for a stab. Knights get flung further the more hurt they are. Three hearts and you're out for a couple of seconds.
WeaponsWeapon pads hand out a Bow, a Bomb Bag or the Custard Cannon. Grab the same one again to level it up. Crates break and sometimes hide a power-up. Watch for pits, spike traps, ice, conveyor belts, lava, mud, springs and portals.
ChaosThe Chaos Meter fills with time and knockouts and unlocks sillier power-ups: Sensible (Zoomy Boots, Bubble Shield, Long Sword, Pork Pie, Magnet Mitts, Prickly Armour), Silly (Giant Head, Bouncy Arms, Swap-o-matic, Banana Trail, Ghost, Bee Swarm, Pie Traps) and Unhinged (Chicken Party, Custard Flood, Boss Mode, Disco Fever, Tiny Town, Meteor Shower, Lights Out, Jelly Arena). Every half a minute the arena itself kicks off: trapdoors, a pie catapult, a chicken stampede, gales, slow-mo, bounties, supply drops. Once a match Steve's golden egg drops. Now and then a shepherd's pie appears: eat it and feel the power of Norr.
