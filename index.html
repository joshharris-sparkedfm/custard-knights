Custard Knights

Eight knights, one arena full of traps. The power-ups start sensible and get sillier every minute until someone turns everyone into chickens.

Weapon pads hand out a Bow, a Bomb Bag or the Custard Cannon. Grab the same one again to level it up. Raise your shield to block swords and bounce arrows back at the shooter.
SensibleZoomy Boots, Bubble Shield, Long Sword, Pork Pie
SillyGiant Head, Bouncy Arms, Swap-o-matic, Banana Trail, Ghost
UnhingedChicken Party, Custard Flood, Boss Mode, Disco Fever, Mirror Curse, Tiny Town

Watch for pits, spike traps that pop every few seconds, ice and conveyor belts. Crates break and sometimes hide a power-up. Shouts fade with distance as a stand-in for proximity chat.

Controls

Just me: move WASD/Arrows, swing or fire Space/J, dash Shift/K, block E/L, shout 1–4
Player 1: WASD, swing F, dash G, block H, shout 1–4
Player 2: Arrows, swing K, dash L, block J, shout 7–0
Touch: stick on the left, Swing, Dash and Block on the right
Tip: dashing carries you over pits
Pause: P or Esc
