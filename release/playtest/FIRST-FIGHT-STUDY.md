# Optional first-fight comparisons

Beta.6 prepares CK-05 for an observed solo-newcomer playtest. It does not supply human findings or change the normal Quick brawl flow. A tester explicitly enables local reporting and starts a test round from Settings. It is offline, solo and optional. Nothing uploads automatically; the local JSON export accompanies their separate written feedback.

## Keep each comparison separate

| Comparison | Random assignment | Held constant |
|---|---|---|
| Arena | Courtyard versus normal random arena selection | Eight total knights, one human, Easy bots, 90 seconds, Simple chaos and existing hints |
| Density | Four versus eight total knights | Courtyard, one human, Easy bots, 90 seconds, Simple chaos and existing hints |

The random arena may itself be Courtyard. Retain that assignment and record the actual map; do not reroll inconvenient results. No variant gets a special weapon, extra reward, new HUD or altered combat rules. These are individual-factor comparisons, not the entire proposed curated tutorial redesign.

An assignment is reused after abandonment while it remains in the local report. A completed comparison cannot be repeated while its completion is retained. Clear, opt-out, storage loss, another installation or eventual truncation can permit reenrollment. This is not participant identity enforcement. The facilitator must track prior participation separately and avoid presenting repeated experienced attempts as first visits.

Normal game configuration is restored after completion or leaving. Test rounds do not award progression or consume the ordinary beginner-start counter. A tester may freely stop, decline the recognition question, or select another normal round. Reporting can be turned off and erased independently of game saves.

## Session procedure and interpretation

Recruit solo newcomers and record whether they have already played any version. Use the same build, input device class and invitation wording within a comparison. Give each newcomer one comparison, assigned before the observed first fight. Do not run both comparisons sequentially and treat the second as a fresh first visit. There is no claim of group-level assignment or social-cohort validity from this solo tool.

Observe whether they can point out their own knight, describe a death and perform an intentional action without coaching. After the round, the optional recognition response records what the tester says, not a measured recognition test. Collect facilitator observations separately from that response. Ask about confusing moments and empty-feeling or overwhelming battles in the offline feedback form.

Before collecting results, choose the primary observation: own-knight recognition for density; purposeful action and actual voluntary next-round start for arena selection. Compare abandonment, inability to act and confusing deaths as guardrails. Report counts, retained denominators and missing outcomes; do not turn a tiny convenience sample into statistical lift or population retention. Assignment and outcomes are recorded by build and variant. A first attack input is not a confirmed hit. A click expressing rematch intent is not an actual next-round start.

The existing roadmap's eight solo newcomers can reveal problems but cannot establish a precise causal effect. Group play, controllers, WAN, minimum hardware and longer-term return require their own acceptance sessions. CK-05 is only accepted after actual observation and reviewed findings; automated fixtures establish engineering behavior alone.
