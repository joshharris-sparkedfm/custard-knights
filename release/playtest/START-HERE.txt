OPTIONAL CONTROLS PRACTICE

The first-launch welcome lets you adjust music, sounds, screen shake and
flashing before playing. Learn by playing covers movement, swing, guard,
dash and bow aim in a safe solo arena. It grants no coins or goals. Pause
with Escape / P / Start to retry or leave; your arena rules and couch seats
return afterwards. Jump straight in skips practice. Existing profiles go
straight to the home menu. How to Play > Learn by playing replays it anytime.

Optional newcomer study: if Joshua asks you to try a first-fight comparison,
go to Options, enable Local play report, select First arena or Number of
knights under Optional first-fight test, then Start test round. It lasts
90 seconds, uses Easy bots and Simple chaos, and grants no coins or goals.
Your normal setup is restored afterwards. The own-knight question is optional.
Try it before other matches and tell Joshua if you have played before.
Clearing the report allows reenrollment; it does not make you a new tester.

Optional: before playing, Options > Local play report lets you record match
and connection observations on this device. It starts OFF and never uploads.
Afterwards, Export report JSON and send that separate file with your feedback
if you wish. Clear removes recorded data; switching reporting off erases it.
This does not change your game saves or require an account.

Do not launch from inside the ZIP or move the EXE away from its sibling files.
Keep older builds in separate folders instead of overwriting.

TRY THIS FIRST — ABOUT 20 MINUTES

Please try without coaching first. Getting stuck is useful feedback.
- Play two Quick Brawls. Try a quick swing, a charged sword swing, blocking
  and dashing. For a charge, keep holding after the first swing until your
  knight starts charging, then release. Tell us what felt satisfying or confusing.
- Open Story (The Great Pudding War), choose Begin at the bakery for a fresh
  story. Try the delivery and pantry rescue, then follow the cooling route
  through the bowl diversion and playable homecoming.
- Open Quick Brawl > Solo Custard Cup for three rounds, or Faction Front; set Army size to
  4 vs 4, then choose Play with bots.
- Change your outfit or visor in Wardrobe, restart, and check it stayed saved.
- Listen to the music in a match and on the results screen. Tell us if it is
  distracting, too loud against the action, or has an awkward ending/repeat.
You can explore more afterwards. Start on Easy or Medium; STEVE is the
hardest setting. All four difficulty levels still need human feedback.

PLAYER FEEDBACK CHANGES

Mouse aim and click attacks work alongside keyboard movement.
Ranged weapons now keep movement separate from mouse or right-stick aim.
A visible aim cue shows the active pointer or controller direction in arena
and Faction Front play. A neutral right stick retains its last aim; keyboard
movement can take facing again. Please report confusing aim or input switching.
Chicken Race pecks shove opponents instead of knocking racing chickens out.
Repeated weapon pickups retain levels through ammo exhaustion and swaps.
Weapons and Long Sword reach cap at level III and reset on defeat.
Arena setup and Couch party offer Classic or Roomier arenas. Roomier has
about 21% more floor space with unchanged movement speed and weapon reach.
Use the same beta version for online Roomier matches. Story and the optional
first-fight study use Classic geometry.

RETAINED FROM BETA 4

Dash/knockback and delayed online snapshots should no longer leave a knight
permanently off-screen. Spawns recheck hazards and occupied positions.
Arena pickups now include Crossbow and Returning Croissant.
Choose Simple, Normal or Insane chaos in arena setup, independently of bots.
Simple keeps basic pickups and ordinary weapons, without escalating events.
Normal builds gradually; Insane starts higher and builds faster.
In eligible Normal/Insane arena rounds, Oh Nae Nae and McGinley each have a 1%
chance of being selected. At most one appears, and many sessions will see neither.
Oh Nae Nae gives one living human an AK47 with 30 shots for up to 12 seconds,
accompanied by the new song. Hold Swing to fire. A new weapon ends it early.
McGinley gives one living human up to eight lightning strikes over 10 seconds.
Walls, guards and spawn protection still count. Death or falling ends either
power. These surprises are off in Simple, races, Story and Faction Front.
Please report if a power or its song remains after the event or a rematch.

REMAPPABLE GAMEPLAY CONTROLS — BETA 13

Open Options > Remap gameplay controls. Choose Solo keyboard, Keyboard half
1 or 2, or Player 1–4 controller, then select the movement/combat bindings.
Controller profiles follow the knight's logical player seat, not the pad's
hardware number. A solo online guest uses Solo keyboard / Player 1 controller.
Solo keyboard applies to solo Quick Brawl, Custom brawl, Story and practice;
joined keyboard halves use their own profiles, even when half 1 plays alone.

Changes remain a draft until Apply. Cancel keeps your current controls.
Reset all to defaults prepares a draft; choose Apply to save that reset.
Conflicting assignments disable Apply and explain which binding to change.
Default actions may have aliases; choosing one key/button replaces that
whole action's aliases. Follow the updated setup, lobby, How to Play and
practice prompts. After applying, release held keys, sticks and buttons
before playing again. Controls save locally and should survive a restart.
If the device cannot save, a message says they apply only for this session.

Remapping covers Arena, Custard Cup, Story and controls practice. Faction
Front keeps its own fixed controls below. Mouse actions, controller movement
and aim axes/d-pad, menu controls, keyboard join F/J and keyboard shout
1–4 / 7–0 stay fixed. Menus use arrows / Tab, Enter / Space, Escape / P;
controller stick / d-pad moves, A picks, B goes back, Start opens the menu.

Try a changed keyboard action and controller action, Cancel a draft, reset
and Apply, then restart to check persistence. Tell us the selected profile,
old/new binding, mode, player seat and whether a held input or a conflict
message was confusing. Physical pads, Steam Input and human usability still
need playtest feedback.

ROOM AND CUSTARD CUP CONTINUITY — BETA 14

On this PC (Couch party) keeps fixed Player 1–4 slots when someone chooses
Leave. A vacant Player 2 slot does not rename Player 3 or Player 4, move their
controls or change their outfit. Missing controllers still reserve their
original slot for recovery. Deliberate Leave makes a vacancy instead.

At Cup results, Leave seat removes that player from the current Cup. Their
past points stay visible as a vacant seat; their Ready and arena vote clear.
Remaining connected players choose an arena and ready up before the host
starts the next round. If everyone leaves, use Leave Cup to return safely.
Room rejoin can reclaim a vacant local slot, but that player observes the
current Cup; joining the next fresh Cup starts their points at zero. Late
online arrivals also wait for a fresh Cup. A disconnected guest's current
Cup seat stays vacant; rejoining the room does not restore Cup membership.

Try a middle player's Leave, recovery of a missing pad, two Cups in a row
and a between-round departure. Tell Joshua which player slot and round,
which Ready/vote action you used, and whether the surviving controls/outfits
or past points changed. This applies to supported couch and arena/Cup rooms;
Faction Front remains a separate service. Human/device/WAN testing is open.

CONTROLS (DEFAULTS)

Solo arena/story: WASD or arrows move, mouse aims, left-click or Space
attacks; Shift dashes; right-click or E blocks. With the sword, tap for a
light swing. Keep holding after the first swing until charging starts,
then release for a heavy that breaks guard and knocks rivals farther.
Picked-up weapons fire instead; their on-screen prompts show the action.
P or Esc pauses. F11 toggles fullscreen. Chicken Race uses the same
attack controls to peck: face a nearby rival to shove them.
Arena/story controller: left stick moves, right stick aims, A swings,
B dashes, X blocks, Start pauses. Follow each mode's on-screen prompts.
Faction Front uses different controls: WASD/arrows move, mouse aims,
click/J attacks, L/Shift heavy attacks, right-click/K guards, Space dashes,
E uses your role ability. Controller: left/right sticks move/aim,
A attacks, B dashes, LT guards, RT heavy attacks, X uses your role ability.
Couch play: up to four seats. Keyboard player one uses WASD and F/G/H
(swing/dash/block), with mouse aim/click fire/right-click block. Player two
uses arrows and J/K/L. Additional seats need pads. Controller aliases are
A or RT swing/fire, B or LB dash, X/RB/LT block; right stick aims.
A missing controller keeps its knight, seat and outfit reserved. Offline
arena/Cup pauses for recovery; online play continues with only that lost
seat neutral (the Match menu still neutralizes all seats on this PC).
Release sticks and buttons on an unassigned controller, use left/right to
choose the missing knight, then press A to reclaim. Browser controller
numbers can change, so choose your original knight deliberately. Recovery
also offers keyboard takeover when that keyboard is unassigned. Release
held controls after recovery before playing. Recover all missing lobby
controllers before starting. Deliberately leaving makes that logical slot
vacant; surviving players keep their controls and outfits. Rejoining fills
the first vacant slot.
Physical-controller compatibility is part of this test, not yet signed off.

ONLINE TESTING IS OPTIONAL

Play with Friends > Online room uses room codes/invites. Have one person
host and share the code privately. Arena/Cup rooms support up to eight total
players, including the host’s local seats. Each remote guest PC contributes
one player; remote guest multiseat is not supported in this build. Try a
small room before a longer session.
Different-household connectivity is not yet verified: report join failures
and the exact message. Do not change router/firewall settings for this test.

Arena Online room now shows when a connection is pending. Escape or Cancel
cancels it; failed joins offer Retry and a message explaining the problem.
If the host closes or stops sending match updates, guests return to the menu
with a message instead of continuing an abandoned round. Rejoin with a fresh
code; interrupted guest rounds do not grant match rewards. Hosts can continue
when a guest leaves. Use matching beta versions for Roomier arena rooms.
During an online match, Menu or Escape / P / Start opens Match menu. The
simulation continues while all players on this PC stay neutral. Choose
Options, Resume or Leave; a host choosing Close room ends it for guests.
This recovery applies to arena/Custard Cup rooms, not the separate Faction
Front service. Report the exact recovery message and what happened first.

Faction Front > Play online · experimental is a separate service. Unless Joshua supplies a reachable
test server address, use Play with bots. Room codes are optional for casual
faction games. The default localhost address
connects only to your own PC. There is no public faction service or persistent
Steam-ranked service. If your host supplies a player access key, the optional
Private test ladder waits for full human teams and saves ratings on that host.
Use Casual with bots for mixed battles. Do not share access keys with others.
For a scheduled private ladder test, start with 4v4: it needs eight separate
players with host-issued keys; bots do not fill the queue. Larger queues need
40 or 100 players. A disconnect pauses the match while that player's remaining
20-second absence budget runs down; returning does not reset the budget.
Leaving or timing out can forfeit the match. Big bot battles do not prove
100-human play. No party queue or public matchmaking service is included.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Note the mode, difficulty, number of humans/bots, and what you did immediately
before it happened. A short clip or screenshot is helpful if you are happy
to share it. Do not include passwords, private conversations or unrelated files.
Native crashes occurred in development tests and remain under investigation;
report any actual game crash, freeze, lost progress or delayed controls.
For an urgent blocker, send Joshua the build number at the top of this guide,
the shortest steps that repeat it, and the exact error text before trying again.

Saves stay on your PC under %APPDATA%\Custard Knights. Do not delete that
folder to troubleshoot. Keep older builds in separate folders; they share
that save location. This package contains no developer or player save profile.

The feedback page works offline and sends nothing automatically. You choose
what to include and send. Unzipping this kit does not update the game or install
a server. This private beta is for finding problems together; it is not a
finished-game, Steam-release or sales promise.
