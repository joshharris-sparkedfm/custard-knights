TRY THIS FIRST — ABOUT 20 MINUTES

Please try without coaching first. Getting stuck is useful feedback.
- Play two Quick brawls. Try a quick swing, holding a charged swing, blocking
  and dashing. Tell us what felt satisfying or confusing.
- Start The Great Pudding War and try the opening story objectives.
- Try a three-round Custard Cup, or open Faction Front, set Army size to
  4 vs 4, then choose Play with bots.
- Change your outfit or visor in Wardrobe, restart, and check it stayed saved.
- Listen to the music in a match and on the results screen. Tell us if it is
  distracting, too loud against the action, or has an awkward ending/repeat.
You can explore more afterwards. Start on Easy or Medium; STEVE is the
hardest setting. All four difficulty levels still need human feedback.

PLAYER FEEDBACK CHANGES

Mouse aim and click attacks work alongside keyboard movement.
Ranged weapons now keep movement separate from mouse or right-stick aim.
A visible aim cue shows the active pointer or controller direction in arena
and Faction Front play. A neutral right stick retains its last aim; keyboard
movement can take facing again. Please report confusing aim or input switching.
Chicken Race pecks shove opponents instead of knocking racing chickens out.
Repeated weapon pickups retain levels through ammo exhaustion and swaps.
Weapons and Long Sword reach cap at level III and reset on defeat.
Arena setup and Couch party offer Classic or Roomier arenas. Roomier has
about 21% more floor space with unchanged movement speed and weapon reach.
Use the same beta version for online Roomier matches. Story and the optional
first-fight study use Classic geometry.

RETAINED FROM BETA 4

Dash/knockback and delayed online snapshots should no longer leave a knight
permanently off-screen. Spawns recheck hazards and occupied positions.
Arena pickups now include Crossbow and Returning Croissant.
Choose Simple, Normal or Insane chaos in arena setup, independently of bots.
Simple keeps basic pickups and ordinary weapons, without escalating events.
Normal builds gradually; Insane starts higher and builds faster.
In eligible Normal/Insane arena rounds, Oh Nae Nae and McGinley each have a 1%
chance of being selected. At most one appears, and many sessions will see neither.
Oh Nae Nae gives one living human an AK47 with 30 shots for up to 12 seconds,
accompanied by the new song. Hold Swing to fire. A new weapon ends it early.
McGinley gives one living human up to eight lightning strikes over 10 seconds.
Walls, guards and spawn protection still count. Death or falling ends either
power. These surprises are off in Simple, races, Story and Faction Front.
Please report if a power or its song remains after the event or a rematch.

CONTROLS

Solo arena/story: WASD or arrows move, mouse aims, left-click or Space
attacks (hold for heavy); Shift dashes; right-click or E blocks.
P or Esc pauses. F11 toggles fullscreen. Chicken Race uses the same
attack controls to peck: face a nearby rival to shove them.
Arena/story controller: left stick moves, right stick aims, A swings,
B dashes, X blocks, Start pauses. Follow each mode's on-screen prompts.
Faction Front uses different controls: WASD/arrows move, mouse aims,
click/J attacks, L/Shift heavy attacks, right-click/K guards, Space dashes,
E uses your role ability. Controller: left/right sticks move/aim,
A attacks, B dashes, LT guards, RT heavy attacks, X uses your role ability.
Couch play: up to four seats. Two can share a keyboard; the lobby explains
F/G/H for player one and J/K/L for player two. Additional seats need pads.
Physical-controller compatibility is part of this test, not yet signed off.

WHAT YOU CAN PLAY

- Arena: eight modes and six arenas, alone with bots or local friends.
- The Great Pudding War: animated, subtitled scenes and eight encounters.
  The opening now needs three sword hits to break the pantry barricade,
  proximity escort for three cooks, then defence and escape.
  Scenes can be paused, replayed or skipped; there is no recorded voice-over.
- Custard Cup: three linked rounds.
- Faction Front bot practice: Brawl, Capture the Flag and Siege, with
  4v4, 20v20 or 50v50 total slots. Bots fill empty slots.
- Wardrobe, earned cosmetics, goals and saved outfits.
- Eleven new soundtrack candidates plus the existing menu theme. Their
  musical fit and repeat points are still being reviewed. The defeat cue,
  Another Helping, currently has about 1.6 seconds of silence at its end.

ONLINE TESTING IS OPTIONAL

The original arena's Online screen uses room codes/invites. Have one person
host and share the code privately. Try a small room before a longer session.
Different-household connectivity is not yet verified: report join failures
and the exact message. Do not change router/firewall settings for this test.

Faction Front online is a separate service. Unless Joshua supplies a reachable
test server address, use Play with bots. Room codes are optional for casual
faction games. The default localhost address
connects only to your own PC. There is no public faction service or persistent
Steam-ranked service. If your host supplies a player access key, the optional
Private test ladder waits for full human teams and saves ratings on that host.
Use Casual with bots for mixed battles. Do not share access keys with others.
For a scheduled private ladder test, start with 4v4: it needs eight separate
players with host-issued keys; bots do not fill the queue. Larger queues need
40 or 100 players. A disconnect pauses the match while that player's remaining
20-second absence budget runs down; returning does not reset the budget.
Leaving or timing out can forfeit the match. Big bot battles do not prove
100-human play. No party queue or public matchmaking service is included.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Note the mode, difficulty, number of humans/bots, and what you did immediately
before it happened. A short clip or screenshot is helpful if you are happy
to share it. Do not include passwords, private conversations or unrelated files.
Native crashes occurred in development tests and remain under investigation;
report any actual game crash, freeze, lost progress or delayed controls.
For an urgent blocker, send Joshua the build number at the top of this guide,
the shortest steps that repeat it, and the exact error text before trying again.

Saves stay on your PC under %APPDATA%\Custard Knights. Do not delete that
folder to troubleshoot. Keep older builds in separate folders; they share
that save location. This package contains no developer or player save profile.

The feedback page works offline and sends nothing automatically. You choose
what to include and send. Unzipping this kit does not update the game or install
a server. This private beta is for finding problems together; it is not a
finished-game, Steam-release or sales promise.
