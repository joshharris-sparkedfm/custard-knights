# Release reliability, complete-functionality verification and multiplayer scale

Research date: 13 October 2026 (Europe/London). Read-only source audit at commit `937cb80` in `work/custard-knights-plan`. The exported and tested Windows beta.7 is frozen at `6f1300b6f5e7dab9f31b684e60d1cadd2bbc5842`; newer uncommitted bakery work in the original checkout is not that build. No tests rerun, files in the game changed, services deployed, accounts purchased or platform submissions made in this research. Recommendations and numerical acceptance budgets below are proposed project criteria, not Steam requirements or achieved results.

## What is actually ready, and what is required for Steam release?

### Takeaway

The project has a reproducible private Windows beta and substantial version-specific technical evidence. It does not yet have accepted story quality, device/WAN coverage, a live public faction service or Steam release approval; keep a claims-to-evidence inventory so marketing never outruns the build.

### Cited Findings

- Beta.7 includes eight arena modes, six arenas, Classic/Roomier geometry, story/Cup/collection and recent race/control fixes. The 59 packaged entries and 73 friends files are checked; packaged controls, story, arena geometry and Rooftop cases passed. These are recorded technical checks, not human quality acceptance. The checkpoint explicitly retains the user's rejection of story quality and states the revised story treatment is unimplemented. [Current checkpoint](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/release/RESUME-CHECKPOINT.md), [immutable export record](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/release/PREVIEW-BUILD.md).
- Roomier adds 10% length in each axis, approximately 21% floor area, without increasing combat reach or speed. Story, demo and first-fight study remain Classic. Same-machine PeerJS evidence is not WAN acceptance. [Roomier review](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/qa/reviews/roomier-arena-2026-10-12.md).
- Valve requires separate approved store and build checklists, with store submission preceding build submission. A mostly final build should include the advertised features. Coming Soon must be public for at least two weeks before release; approval does not automatically publish the game. [Release process](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/releasing).
- Valve's review page says typical store/build review is 3–5 business days, and asks developers to allow at least seven business days. Store screenshots should be gameplay; launch feature claims must exist in the submitted build; supported operating systems must launch successfully. [Review process](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/review_process).
- Current onboarding documentation says a **21-day** fee-to-release wait for the first few titles, plus the two-week Coming Soon period. Historical project notes mention conflicting 21/30-day wording; do not promise a calendar launch from old notes—confirm the actual account's eligibility and review status in Steamworks. Company, bank/tax and identity onboarding remain owner inputs. [Onboarding](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/onboarding). The app fee is currently USD100 or equivalent; this research authorizes no payment. [Steam Direct fee](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee).
- Current required store capsule sizes: header920×430, small462×174, main1232×706, vertical748×896. At least five actual gameplay screenshots are required. Capsule logos must remain legible; do not substitute concept art or pre-rendered stills for gameplay screenshots. [Store graphical assets](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard).
- A trailer is required. Valve recommends gameplay first, understandable without audio, and showing the player's actual perspective/HUD. Preferred upload: up to1920×1080,30/60fps, high bitrate, H.264/AAC in an accepted container. Treat generated story footage as cinematic footage, not proof of gameplay. [Trailers](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer).
- Steam's Content Survey explicitly concerns AI-generated content shipped to and consumed by players, including sound/art/narrative/localisation. Pre-generated assets and live-generated content have different questions; live generation requires a description of safeguards. Prepare a truthful asset-use disclosure for the installed Suno soundtrack and any shipped generated visuals/writing, with provenance. This is platform preparation, not a legal conclusion. [Content Survey](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey).
- Steam Playtest uses a separate child AppID, can gate access, and requires a simplified store/build review. It provides Steamworks features while keeping testing separate from the main game; it is a possible next distribution stage, not something already enabled. The user's present selection remains the downloadable friends beta. [Steam Playtest](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/playtest).
- Existing unimplemented roadmap work remains explicit: CK09 warning audio/banner priority is partial; CK22 House Rule and attributable chain reaction missing; CK24 runtime sharing/capture and creator pilot partial; CK20 human evidence absent. CK04 local consent-based reporting and CK05 optional study implementation do not supply actual cohort results. [Roadmap ledger](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/release/ROADMAP-STATUS.md), [original done-when criteria](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/BUILD-PLAN.md).

## How do we verify the entire experience without meaningless test-count inflation?

### Takeaway

Test player journeys and failure invariants, then target high-risk combinations. Automated completion establishes that a path can work; only observed people and physical devices establish that players can understand, control and enjoy it.

### Cited Findings

- Arena definitions are Free-for-all, Red vs Blue, Last Knight Standing, King of the Pie, Pie Heist, Chicken Racing, Flag Frenzy and Hot Pie. Their score/lives/flags/race outcomes are genuinely different, so one generic match smoke cannot validate them all. [Mode definitions, index.html](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/index.html#L501).
- The host creates local humans from configured seats plus remote peers up to eight entities; a remote connection maps to exactly one entity, and guest validation requires exactly one matching peer entity. This supports a narrower mixed topology than arbitrary multi-seat households on both ends. Physical acceptance remains unperformed. [Host and guest networking](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/index.html#L2916), [player creation](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/index.html#L626).
- Valve recommends simultaneous mouse/gamepad input, correct device glyphs, menu operation with d-pad/stick/mouse and avoiding double input; it recommends distinct menu action sets. These are controller design recommendations, not evidence that our implementation meets them. [Steam Input developer guide](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/steam_controller/getting_started_for_devs).
- Remote Play Together streams the host's game and makes remote friends behave as local participants; only the host needs the game installed/owned. It is different from native replicated multiplayer. Valve describes private testing before enabling the store feature. [Remote Play](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/remoteplay).
- The existing player-review protocol already separates first-time solo learning, couch/devices, household networks, faction roles/difficulty, and music audition. It identifies startup failures, lost rewards, unclear objectives and persistent stalls as release concerns. No observations have yet been recorded. [Player review](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/release/PLAYER-REVIEW.md).
- The local native Node access violations and Python anomalies remain unexplained; hosted CI success does not establish their cause or repair them. Earlier stable browser runs also cannot close a distinct failed test process. [Stability review](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/qa/reviews/sales-readiness-2026-10-09.md). This historical review's missing-music text is superseded by the current roadmap: all13cues now exist. [Current music status](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/release/ROADMAP-STATUS.md).

**Coverage strategy:** run invariant modules for changed rules, one input-only end-to-end journey for each distinct mode/terminal state, all6×2mapgeometry checks, then pairwise device/difficulty/chaos/map combinations. Add explicit3-way hotspots: Rooftop+race+Roomier; ice+dash+knockback; online+weaponupgrade+death;4seats+padreconnect+Cup; cinematic+pause+controllerfocus; rarepower+disconnect+rematch. Every mode must get a human complete match, but do not run the enormous full Cartesian product as a ritual. Reuse frozen passing evidence when code and its dependencies are unchanged; a new build needs affected regressions and a small packaged smoke, not re-reporting old tests as fresh.

**Severity policy (proposal):** P0 crash/data loss/security/unsafe disclosure, P1 blocked progression/input/objective/network results or repeatable severe stall, P2 material confusion/readability/balance, P3 cosmetic. Zero open P0/P1 for every advertised mode before candidate release; P2 needs fix or explicit accepted limit with reproduction/evidence. A reproducible native fault needs a bounded investigation under a new hypothesis (runtime/OS/driver isolation, crash dump, independenthost reproduction); never rerun until lucky then close it. If confined to tooling prove that boundary; if reproduced in packaged game/service it blocks release.

**Human fun study (proposed, not statistical proof):** first small batch6–8newcomers solo and3–4friendgroups; later retest changed experiences with fresh people. Measure time to deliberate firstaction, unaided menu navigation, objective/defeat explanation, voluntary nextmatch/chapter, stoppingreason, frustration incidents, faction/character recognition and delayed return. Separate solo, couch, nativeonline and streamed cohorts. Suggested product gates: ≥80% independently reach/play intended firstmode within90seconds; ≥80% explain currentgoal and onecombatcounter after firstmatch; majority voluntarily continue when freely offered an exit; no recurring blocker in two people. These are team decision thresholds, not universal norms or retention guarantees. Ratings/heatmaps do not replace watching behaviour. Existing optional local reports remain consented/defaultoff, exclude QA/demo/bots, and never upload silently.

**CK09 implementation acceptance:** explicit priority arbitration for criticalhazard/lastlife/objective vsreward/flavour banners; bounded soundvoices with criticalwarnings reserved; music/effectducking with recovery; representativeoverlaps audibly/visuallyunambiguous; preservation of reducedmotion/flash settings. **CK22:** one deterministic boundedHouseRule with hostauthority/tiebreak/default and visibleprematch explanation; chainreaction records instigator/triggersequence and nofakeawards; compare normalrules withpeople before retention. **CK24:** opt-in postmatch postcard/shortcapture of actualgameplay, costbudget/disableoption, playernameprivacy, localexport; tiny creatorrecipientpilot measures activation without inventing referrals. All remain roadmap work, notclaimed done.

### Gaps

- No physical-device, mixed-household or streamed-session evidence supplied. Existing source supports hostlocal+remote, but guestmulti-seat households require design/implementation; factionUI currently has one localId/camera, so factioncouch is not established.
- No human fun study, delayedreturn data or soundtrack approval. Proposed thresholds require actual observation and may need revision after the first cohort.
- No complete platform/OS/minspec inventory can be asserted before machines are selected and measured.

## How should100vs100 and public online play be developed and accepted?

### Takeaway

The source implements 4v4/20v20/50v50: 8/40/100 total combatants, including bots. The requested 100v100 is 200 total and requires a new capacity/readability/operations milestone; neither bots nor a hundred loopback sockets prove hundreds of human internet players.

### Cited Findings

- Faction code has Custardia/Rice, Vanguard/Ranger/Engineer/Support, brawl/CTF/siege and fourdecisionprofiles. Client state validation currently limits players to100; deltas carry player attack/state and worldobjective/effect arrays. [Factioncore](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/game/mass-battle.js), [UI/validation](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/game/mass-battle-ui.js), [wireformat](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/game/mass-battle-wire.js).
- A 60-second separate-process 100-socket workload delivered approximately 20 broadcasts/sec and 205–212 Mbps aggregate application payload (2.05–2.12 Mbps per recipient, excluding TCP/TLS/WebSocket overhead). Client input timing failed on all three hosts and Linux had a 1.77-second worst receive gap; no decode errors/disconnections is not smoothness acceptance. Earlier shared-process evidence reported 122–132 Mbps at only 12–12.5 delivered snapshots/sec. Different delivery rates and match evolution explain why these are different workload observations, not a measured bandwidth regression or a new guaranteed capacity. [Process-load evidence and comparison](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/qa/reviews/battle-process-load-2026-10-09.md).
- A later oneheadlessbrowser+99scriptedclients samehost sample measured59.93FPS,20.04receiveHz,0.4msdecodep95 and69.5msmaximumreceivegap; camera included spawnarea, not permanentcentralcongestion. This is supporting diagnostic evidence, notWAN/minspec/100human proof. [Browserload review](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/qa/reviews/sales-readiness-2026-10-09.md).
- The private ladder has individual authenticatedqueues, rating/database/resultjournal and cumulative20second disconnectgrace; a disconnect pauses the entire battle, reservesslot, and expiry forfeits the whole faction. Parties, publicSteamidentity, competitivecalibration andanti-collusion remain unfinished. Live battles/queues are memory-only; serverrestart does not resume them. [Private ladder](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/release/PRIVATE-LADDER.md).
- Current deployment is loopback bydefault, nonroot/read-only container with512MiB limit, optionalCaddyTLS and privateSQLite overlays. No public endpoint exists; healthcheck is not an automatic unhealthy-process restart. [Deploymentguide](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/deploy/README.md), [Compose](https://github.com/joshharris-sparkedfm/custard-knights/blob/937cb80/deploy/compose.yaml).
- Valve's server-auth flow uses a client-generated ticket, waits for itscallback, sends it to a securebackend, and verifies it against the app using the Steam WebAPI. The verified identity is a SteamID; ownership checks are separately described. Private bearerkeys are not Steamownership. [Steam authentication](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/auth).
- Epic's official networking guidance explains authoritative state, dedicatedserver advantages for large/trusted games, and perconnection relevance/priority to limit replication. These are transferable design principles, **not a recommendation to rewrite our game in Unreal or evidence of our capacity**. [Networking overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/networking-overview-for-unreal-engine), [replication graph](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/replication-graph-in-unreal-engine).
- Browser WebSocket lacks built-in backpressure; excess incomingmessages can accumulate memory or saturate CPU. Our protocol therefore needs explicitbounded queues/coalescing/resync policies and evidence under slowclients. Changing transports is not automatically necessary. [MDN WebSocket](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebSockets_API).

### Inferences

**Staged implementation and rollout, each gate recorded:**

1. **8 total:** authoritative invariants, all three objectives/four roles, bot fill/replacement, reconnect/result rules, physical inputs and one real regional host. First human WAN 4v4 with known latency; define fair team/party expectations before public ranked.
2. **40 total:** 20v20 scripted clients on separate hosts plus actual rendered clients; worst camera/central melee, all roles/projectiles/abilities. Then supervised human/bot mix and an actual 40-human event if claiming 40 humans. Record both counts independently.
3. **100 total:** 50v50 protocol/budget checks on intended host/minimum client, mixed human-bot group, then a scheduled 100-human public-service test. Confirm all three modes and full match duration, not only short joins/load. Validate cost, regional allocation and capacity shedding.
4. **200 total:** implement version-negotiated capacity across core/UI/validation/wire/server/queue/results; world layout, safe spawns, route width and objective density; spatial broadphase, AI decision budgets, server interest sets and near/far replication priorities; bounded projectiles/effects, render culling/cache budget. Preserve full-state/resync atomicity and critical objective visibility. Then 200 bots, 200 distributed scripted connections, mixed humans, actual 200-human sessions. Make the release claim only after this gate. Benchmark against 100 with the same seed/setup; never extrapolate a pass.
5. **Operations before broad promotion:** owned DNS/WSS certificates/exact origins, server authentication, capacity/rate limits, metrics/alerting, roster/result durability, restore drill, blue/green or declared-maintenance rollout, incident owner/support. Server process restart success is not live-match recovery. Add party roster/leader transfer/queue cancellation/rejoin contracts; public ranked requires calibrated matchmaking and an abuse policy.

**Important gameplay inference:** pausing100 or200people whenever one player disconnects is likely disruptive and exploitable. Evaluate a distinct scalable publicpolicy (bounded individualreservation/rejoin, teamabandonmentrules, unratedabortthresholds, clearcommunication) withplayers. Preserve current private ladder policy until deliberate versioned replacement; never silently alter awardedresults. Reject stale/forged/replayed inputs, impossible movement/fire rates, duplicateidentities, partyqueue manipulation, wintrading anddisconnect grief; provide operator audit, sanctions and appeal/support path. These require realoperatingdecisions, not a claim thatserverauthority is complete anticheat.
